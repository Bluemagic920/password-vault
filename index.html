Create your vault

Choose one master password. It encrypts everything in this vault, and it never leaves this device. Next you will get a recovery key, which resets the master password if you forget it.

Four or five unrelated words make a strong password that is easy to remember.

Save your recovery key

This key is the only way to reset your master password. Write it down or print it, and keep it somewhere outside this vault. It will not be shown again.

If you lose both your master password and this key, nobody can open the vault.
